[[file-a-paper]]
= How to file and summarize a paper

This guide shows you how to file a paper's PDF in the knowledge base, write its summary, and turn its ideas into zettels.

. File the PDF and create its note:
+
[source,console]
----
$ ztk paper new ~/Downloads/lamport78.pdf \
    --title "Time, Clocks, and the Ordering of Events" \
    --venue cacm --year 1978 --authors "Leslie Lamport" \
    --link https://example.org/lamport78
Filed paper 'Time, Clocks, and the Ordering of Events' with its PDF at ~/notes/papers/cacm/1978/time-clocks-and-the-ordering-of-events.pdf.
~/notes/papers/cacm/1978/time-clocks-and-the-ordering-of-events.md
----
+
ztk copies the PDF and leaves the original where it was.
Only `--title` is required: without `--venue` the paper goes under `others`, and without `--year` under the current year.
Add `--editor` to open the note right away.

. Fill in the note's sections.
Each starts with a line in italics saying what it holds; replace it with your own words.

. For each idea worth keeping on its own, create a zettel:
+
[source,console]
----
$ ztk new Happened-before relation
----
+
In the zettel, link back to the paper by its note's name: `[[time-clocks-and-the-ordering-of-events]]`.

. Link the zettels from the task notes that apply them.

To find the paper again later, search for it by its type:

[source,console]
----
$ ztk search clocks --type paper
----
